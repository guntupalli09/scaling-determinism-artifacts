# Corpus independence — what can and cannot be claimed

Status: 2026-10-17. All facts below are from the Git history and the dataset sources, verified
by `validate_day1.py` (runs/validation/).

## 1. The planned 2026 Q3 MCC holdout cannot currently be executed

- The design proposed a held-out set of MCC 2026 Q3 filings (Jul–Sep 2026), which no ruleset
  version could have seen.
- **The MCC hasn't published them yet.** The index manifest (`/index/manifest.json`) was generated
  2026-08-06T22:59Z, and its newest quarter is 2026 Q2. Checked 2026-10-16.
- MCC exports can only be started from its web app. Scripted export requests are refused ("Exports
  can only be started from the web app."), and I did not try to get around that control.
- **No substitute has been made.** 2025 filings were *not* swapped in. Changing the holdout would
  be a design change for Prof. Laakkonen to decide.
- Consequence: no result in this package is a Q3-holdout result.

## 4. Claims

**Supported:**
- "Evaluated on documents that were not present in the repository when the evaluated ruleset was
  frozen, and that do not overlap the development fixtures." This applies to v1.0.3–v7.2.0 on all
  slices, and to the post-corpus states on the CUAD and ContractNLI slices only.
- "Expert-labelled third-party datasets (CUAD, ContractNLI) that were never used in rule
  development, according to the repository history."

**Not supported (do not use):**
- "Never seen" or "unseen". The MCC Q1–Q2 exhibits were publicly available on EDGAR before the
  baseline was frozen, and CUAD and ContractNLI have been public since 2021. Repository history
  cannot exclude informal exposure of the author to those public documents.
- "Out-of-distribution" in a strong sense. The MCC slices are the same genre as the development
  data (US SEC-filed commercial agreements). Their distribution differs from the synthetic
  development set, but no formal distribution-shift measurement was made.
- "Held-out Q3 2026 evaluation". Not executed (Section 1).
- Any MCC-slice claim about the post-corpus 7.2.0 states.
